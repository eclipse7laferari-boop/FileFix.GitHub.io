Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to FileFix. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our website and online tools.

2. Information We Collect

FileFix does not currently require users to create accounts or provide personal information to use its image compression tools.

We do not directly collect uploaded images or send them to our own servers.

3. Image Processing

Image compression is performed directly in your browser using JavaScript and the HTML Canvas API.

Your original images remain on your device during processing and are not uploaded to FileFix servers.

4. Local Storage

FileFix uses browser storage technologies, including Local Storage and IndexedDB, to save compression history and related information.

This information is stored locally on your device. You can remove it by clearing your browser data.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information when visitors access the website.

FileFix also uses the JSZip library, delivered through a third-party CDN, to create ZIP archives.

6. Advertising

FileFix may display advertisements from third-party advertising providers, including Google AdSense.

Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to display and measure advertisements.

Learn more about Google's advertising practices: Google Advertising Policies .

7. Cookies

FileFix currently uses browser storage for website functionality. If third-party advertising or analytics services are introduced, additional cookies or similar technologies may be used.

8. Children's Privacy

FileFix is a general-purpose website and is not specifically directed at children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as FileFix develops or introduces new features.

Any changes will be published on this page.
