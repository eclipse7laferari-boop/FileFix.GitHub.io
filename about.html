About FileFix

Simple tools. Better workflow.

Who We Are

FileFix is a free online platform designed to make working with digital files easier and more accessible.

We are building a collection of simple tools that help people manage images and documents without complicated software.

Our Mission

Our goal is to make everyday file tasks simple, fast and convenient for everyone.

We believe useful online tools should be easy to understand and accessible from different devices.

What We Offer

FileFix is developing tools for:

  • Image compression
  • Image resizing and conversion
  • PDF management
  • Other useful file operations

More tools will be introduced as the platform grows.

Our Approach to Privacy

Our image compression tool processes images directly in your browser.

We aim to keep our tools simple and transparent about how they work.

Read our Privacy Policy for more information.
